Recording, Mixing and Mastering Studio

Carly Road
Studio

Carly Road Studio is the recording studio where Carly Yeah’s songs have taken shape.

A home studio connected to the project from the beginning, where the six songs on Still Looking Up were recorded.

Carly Yeah is the artist; Carly Road Records, the independent label; and Carly Road Studio, the recording space. Three parts of the same project, brought together by the desire to make music and share it.

Carly Road’s Garage

Selected work

Carly Road Studio brings together a selection of Carly Yeah’s work in production, recording, editing, mixing, mastering, instrumental performance and programming. These projects include Mi Tío Jimmy, Marcos del Valle and Good Bye Lenin. Each release is listed with its technical and artistic credits, in order of publication.
