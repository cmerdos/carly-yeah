About Still Looking Up

Still Looking Up is the debut release by Carly Yeah. Six songs written at different moments but connected by the same way of looking forward: accepting what has happened, living with questions that remain unanswered and continuing to move even when not everything is clear.

The EP starts from rock but moves freely through folk, pop and alternative elements. Guitars and melodies sit at the centre, while the lyrics explore relationships, loss, memories, decisions, farewells and the need to find a next step.

Boreal, No Reason Why, This Is My Stop, Let Me Be, What the Hell (After Summer) and When We Let Go do not tell a single story, but they share a perspective. Each song captures a different moment and, together, they form a journey between what is left behind and what may still lie ahead.

An independent project

Still Looking Up was created and recorded independently in a home studio. The project comes after many years of writing and making music, but marks the point at which those songs begin to be released under the Carly Yeah name.

The production, recording and development of the release follow the same philosophy behind the project itself: making music freely, taking care with every song and sharing it without trying to turn it into something different from what it was originally meant to be.

The full track listing, release status and listening links remain in the Music section of the main Carly Yeah website so the EP is not duplicated across two different pages.
